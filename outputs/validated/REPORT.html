Research question

How much information about the supplied default outcome remains when outcome-adjacent fields and dataset-provided risk estimates are removed? The answer is evaluated on later origination cohorts, with all fitted preprocessing restricted to training data.

Design and validation

Training: 39,897 loans originated before 2022-03-01. Holdout: 10,103 loans originated on or after that date. The models are prespecified; neither is selected or tuned using these results. The 95% AUC intervals use 200 bootstrap resamples of the same holdout.

Feature allowlist: credit_score, ead, coupon_rate, leverage, interest_coverage, debt_to_equity, maturity_months, sector, loan_type, collateral, initial_rating.

Excluded fields: pd_annual, lgd, el, unexpected_loss, rwa, default_date, survival_months, recovery_rate, loss_given_default. The loan identifier, dates, and outcome flag are also excluded as predictors.

Expected-loss sensitivity

EL = Σ exposure × stressed annual PD × stressed LGD. These scenarios use the supplied synthetic annual PD and LGD, separately from the classifiers. Both risk inputs are capped at 100%. Shocks are assumptions, not calibrated forecasts.

What this demonstrates

Auditable feature selection, preprocessing isolated from the holdout, reproducible cohort partitioning, uncertainty reporting, and a clear separation between classification scores and annual expected loss. The modest results are more useful than near-perfect scores obtained from permissive feature sets.

Limits of the evidence

  • Synthetic outcomes do not establish real-world predictive ability.
  • Chronological split is by origination cohort. Outcome timestamps and observation horizons are not validated, so this is not a historical deployment backtest.
  • The target is the supplied default flag across varied loan lifetimes, not a calibrated one-year probability of default.
  • Stress results use supplied annual PD and LGD with illustrative shocks; they are separate from classifier scores.
  • No model is selected or tuned on the holdout. Both prespecified models are reported.
  • Bootstrap AUC intervals reflect resampling of the holdout only.

Existing project describes a Kaggle dataset; original listing and redistribution license were not recorded. Raw rows are not included in the public site.

Reproduce the result

Run python run_analysis.py from the creditrisk repository. Tests: python -m pytest tests -q. The summary JSON, holdout predictions, sector aggregates, and stress assumptions are generated in outputs/validated/.
